Musical Instruments are one of lifes greatest gifts.

But what are the main of instruments people use?

  • Brass insteruments: Tuba, Euphonium, Baritone, Trombone, French Horn, Trumpet.
  • Woodwind Instruments: Flute, Clarinet, Alto and Tenor Saxophone etc.
  • Percussion instruments: Bass Drum, Tenor Drum, Flub Drum, Cymbals, Snare Drum.
  1. String Instruments: Contra, Viola, Cello, Violin
  2. Non-traditional String Instruments: Piano, Keyboard, Autoharp, Sarangi etc.
  3. Bell Instruments: Chime, Handbells, Carillon, Cowbell, Sleigh Bells etc.
